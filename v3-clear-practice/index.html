Sound familiar?

You can name the pattern. You can tell the story. It still hasn’t shifted.

Most people who come to me already understand a lot about themselves. They may even know exactly where it came from. But with trauma, chronic stress, grief or anxiety, something in the system is often still holding it.

  • You’ve had therapy, and it helped, but something deeper hasn’t moved
  • You feel tight, flooded, shut down or stuck in ways talking can’t reach
  • You want spiritual or existential material taken seriously

How I work

Begin with what’s actually here, so it can be met rather than managed.

At the centre is Focusing, developed by Eugene Gendlin through research with Carl Rogers into what helps therapy lead to change: the ability to pause, sense into lived experience, and stay with something before it is fully formed.

I also draw on Evidence-Based EFT, IFS parts work and somatic approaches. They sit inside one larger process. The work is never about applying a method to you.

FAQ

Your questions, answered.

Do sessions work online?

Yes. Most sessions are held online, and clients regularly say they feel just as understood and guided over Zoom. In-person sessions are on Harley Street, London.

Do I need to have done therapy before?

No, though many clients have. This work often suits people who found talking therapy helpful but noticed something deeper hadn’t shifted.

Is there a lower-cost option?

Sliding-scale places are available for 1:1 sessions, and payment plans and scholarships are available for trainings. Email Mitch to ask.

How does EBEFT accreditation work?

After the practitioner training, accreditation involves mentoring, case studies and an ethics exam. Training for organisations is available at a negotiated price.
