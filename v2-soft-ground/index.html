My approach

You can name the pattern. You can tell the story.

Most people who come to me already understand a lot about themselves. They may even know exactly where it came from. But insight has not yet become change.

That is often the case with trauma, chronic stress, grief, anxiety, or experiences that were too much, too fast, or too long carried alone. Something in the system is still holding it.

1

We begin with what is here

What your body is holding, what your nervous system is doing, what feels tight, flooded, shut down, or hard to reach.

2

We make contact

With what is carrying the distress, so it can be met properly rather than managed or worked around.

3

Something begins to move

Drawing on Focusing, Evidence-Based EFT, IFS parts work and somatic approaches, inside one larger process.
