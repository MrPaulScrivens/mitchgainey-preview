My approach

They can name the pattern. They can tell the story.

Most people who come to me already understand a lot about themselves. They may even know exactly where it came from. But insight has not yet become change.

That is often the case with trauma, chronic stress, grief, anxiety, or experiences that were too much, too fast, or too long carried alone. Something in the system is still holding it.

At the centre of my work is Focusing, a body-based approach developed by Eugene Gendlin through research with Carl Rogers into what actually helps therapy lead to change. Change does not come from insight alone. It comes when a person can pause, sense into their own lived experience, and stay with something there before it is fully formed or explained.

I begin with what is actually here: what your body is holding, what your nervous system is doing, what feels tight, flooded, shut down, conflicted, or hard to reach. From there, I help you come into contact with what is carrying the distress, so it can be met properly rather than managed or worked around.

“The work is not about applying a method to you. It is about helping something in you come into the kind of contact that allows movement.”On how I work

People who have done enough talking

This work tends to suit people who are thoughtful, self-aware, and looking for something more than generic advice or surface-level coping. It is often a good fit if you have had therapy before and know that, while it helped, something deeper has not shifted.

  • Trauma & chronic overwhelm
  • Shutdown & anxiety
  • Grief
  • Patterns held in the body
  • Spiritual & existential material
  • Practitioners seeking depth
02

Fifteen years, under real pressure

I have worked with homeless young people, survivors of torture and trauma from refugee backgrounds, Indigenous communities, and professionals in high-risk environments. It has required what I offer to be practical, grounded, and able to hold up under pressure, not just theory.

  • Accredited Inner Relationship Focusing Teacher
  • Evidence-Based EFT practitioner & trainer
  • IFS Level 1
  • Somatic Experiencing (in training)
  • B. Community & Social Studies (Counselling)
  • Adv. Diploma in Counselling

I am not a psychologist and I do not diagnose mental health conditions. I work within a clear scope of practice with ongoing supervision.
