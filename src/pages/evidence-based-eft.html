About the training

EFT is often taught as a technique. Here, it is taught as part of a larger process.

Who is it for?

This training is suited to:

  • therapists, psychologists, and counsellors
  • coaches and practitioners
  • health professionals and support workers
  • anyone wanting a structured, evidence-based approach to working with emotional distress
What you’ll learn
  • how EFT works from a nervous system and research perspective
  • how to use tapping safely and effectively with clients
  • how to work with trauma, anxiety, and emotional intensity
  • how to integrate EFT with other therapeutic approaches
  • how to move beyond scripts into responsive, client-led work
How I teach

I teach EFT as a relational and process-oriented method, not just a protocol.

You will learn the structure, but also how to think, respond, and adapt in real time. The emphasis is on:

  • clinical safety
  • responsiveness
  • depth of work
  • real-world application
Format

Training is online and supported by training assistants.

I have chosen a unique structure to minimise “Zoom fatigue” and maximise learning. Trainings are generally held 9am–4:30pm on a Friday and 9am–1:30pm on a Saturday over two consecutive weeks.

This has had good feedback from participants over the usual three-day Friday-to-Sunday model.

Advanced training

Evidence-Based EFT for Trauma

This two-day training focuses on how to work with trauma using Evidence-Based EFT in a way that is safe, structured, and clinically grounded.

It is designed for practitioners who already have a foundation in EFT and want to deepen their ability to work with more complex presentations, including trauma, overwhelm, and nervous system dysregulation.

Many practitioners learn EFT as a technique, but feel less certain when working with trauma. Questions tend to arise around:

  • how to work safely when intensity increases
  • how to recognise and respond to overwhelm or shutdown
  • how to avoid pushing too quickly or working too directly
  • how to adapt EFT when standard approaches are not enough

This training is designed to address those gaps. It is held over two days, online.

Prerequisites

To attend the training, you must have attended the practitioner training with a minimum gap of three months, unless you have made prior arrangements with Dr Peta Stapleton.

What you will learn

Across the two days, we will cover:

  • how trauma presents in the body and nervous system
  • how to pace EFT work appropriately with trauma
  • how to recognise signs of overwhelm, dissociation, and shutdown
  • how to regulate activation without suppressing process
  • how to work with specific memories without re-traumatisation
  • how to use EFT alongside a process-oriented, client-led approach
  • how to adapt your work when clients cannot access words or clear narratives
How it is taught

This training is grounded in research-informed EFT, but taught within a broader somatic and relational framework. The emphasis is not only on what to do, but on:

  • how to think clinically
  • how to track what is happening in real time
  • how to respond to the person in front of you, rather than applying a fixed protocol

You will learn structure, but also how to work with flexibility, precision, and care.

Our team

Who you’ll be learning from.

Founder of Evidence-Based EFT

Dr Peta Stapleton

Dr Peta Stapleton is a Clinical and Health Psychologist and Professor of Psychology at Bond University, internationally recognised for her research and clinical work in Emotional Freedom Techniques (EFT). She has presented her work at TEDx, authored The Science Behind Tapping (Hay House), and serves on multiple international scientific advisory boards. Her contributions to psychology have been widely recognised through numerous awards, including Psychologist of the Year (2019) and the Australian Psychological Society’s Distinguished Contribution to Psychological Science Award (2024).

EBEFT Trainer & Mentor

Mitch Gainey

Mitch Gainey is a therapeutic practitioner, trainer, and Focusing teacher based in London, working online with clients and practitioners across the UK and Australia. With over fifteen years of experience across trauma, community practice, and leadership development, his work integrates Focusing, Evidence-Based EFT, and somatic approaches within a precise, process-oriented framework. He is the only accredited Evidence-Based EFT trainer currently based in the UK and EU, and mentors practitioners seeking greater depth, clarity, and clinical integrity in their work.
