1:1 Sessions · Online & Harley Street

Working together.

This work is for people who know something needs to change but have not found a way through it yet.

See session optionsAsk a question

You may be capable and self-aware. You may have tried therapy, thinking, or managing it from the surface. But certain patterns remain.

You do not need a category. If something is not moving, we can work with it.

Sessions are one-to-one and work directly with what is present: physical sensation, emotional patterns, parts of you with competing needs. I draw from Focusing, Evidence-Based EFT, parts work, and somatic approaches. The process follows what is actually happening, not a script.

This is not advice. I am not here to tell you what to do. We work at the level where change actually happens, which means staying with experience long enough for it to move on its own terms. Slower at first. More lasting over time.
